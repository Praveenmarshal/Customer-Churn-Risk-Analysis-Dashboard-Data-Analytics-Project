# Customer Churn & Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns to help businesses identify high-risk customers and key factors contributing to attrition. Using interactive Power BI dashboards, the analysis translates raw customer data into actionable insights for decision-makers.

---

## 📊 Dataset
- ~7,000 customer records
- Includes customer demographics, service subscriptions, contract details, payment methods, tenure, and billing information
- Dataset source: Public telecom churn dataset (synthetic / educational)

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics
- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Customers at Risk: 1,869
- Yearly Charges: $16.06M
- Monthly Charges: $139.13K

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Month-to-month contracts show the highest churn rate (~42%)
- Fiber optic internet users contribute the highest revenue but also experience the highest churn
- Customers with tenure less than 1 year are significantly more likely to churn
- Electronic check payment method has the highest churn rate

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Promote long-term contracts with incentives
- Improve service quality and support for fiber optic users
- Target early-tenure customers with onboarding and engagement programs
- Optimize billing and payment experience to reduce friction

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- KPI Design
- Business Intelligence

---

## 📈 Outcome
The dashboard enables stakeholders to:
- Monitor churn and revenue risk in real time
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Make data-driven retention decisions
